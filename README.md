# StintIQ Capture

Downloads for **StintIQ Capture**, a small Windows app that records what your sim (iRacing, Le Mans Ultimate or ACC on PC) shares during a team race. [StintIQ](https://stintiq.app) uses these test recordings to learn exactly what happens during a driver swap.

It only **reads** data the sims publish for tools like overlays and dashboards. It never changes your car, your setup or anything in the race.

**➡ Download the latest version: [Releases](../../releases/latest)** (`StintIQ-Capture.exe`, one file, nothing to install)

## How to use it

1. **Download** `StintIQ-Capture.exe` from the latest release. Save it somewhere easy, like your Desktop.
2. **Double-click it.** Windows will probably say **"Windows protected your PC"**. That's normal for new apps from small developers. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. **Type your name and role**, e.g. `Jake, driving first`, and click **Start recording**.
4. **Start your sim as normal.** The window shows **"● Recording …"**.
   - If a **yellow box** appears, follow it. For ACC, click **Turn it on**, then restart ACC. For LMU, turn on Settings → Gameplay → Enable Plugins, then restart LMU.
5. **Race normally**, including your driver swaps. Leave the window open; you can minimize it.
6. After the race, click **Stop & save**. A zip appears on your **Desktop**.
7. **Upload that zip** with the link your team captain gave you. It's usually too big for a normal Discord message.

## Questions

- **Is it safe with anti-cheat?** It reads only what the sims publish for third-party tools, the same way apps like SimHub and CrewChief do. It doesn't touch the game itself.
- **What does it record?** Timing, car data and driver names from your session. Not your Windows user name, files or anything else on your PC.
- **How big is the zip?** Roughly 15–30 MB per hour of racing.

This repository holds downloads only.
